## How to choose a good dukan ka naam

Before the lists, here are five rules that separate a name customers remember from one they forget:

1. **Easy to say and spell.** If customers can't tell an auto driver or type it into Google Maps, it's too complicated.
2. **Two or three words maximum.** "Shree Ganesh General Store" works; a five-word name won't fit on a board or a UPI display name.
3. **Hints at what you sell.** "Annapurna Kirana" tells you it's groceries; "Rang Mahal" suggests clothes or sarees.
4. **Fits your area and customers.** A traditional name suits an old market; a modern Hinglish name may suit a mall or a college area.
5. **Not already used nearby.** Two "Balaji Mobile" shops on the same road confuse customers and Google.

## Names with a blessing or family touch

Many Indian shop owners like to include a deity, a family name or an auspicious word. Done well, this builds instant trust in local markets:

| Style | Examples | Works best for |
|---|---|---|
| Auspicious word + category | Shubh Labh Traders, Mangalam Sarees | Traditional markets |
| Family name + category | Agarwal Kirana, Sharma Electronics | Established family businesses |
| Deity + category | Shree Krishna Dairy, Sai Medicals | Neighbourhood shops |
| Modern Hinglish | Gadget Adda, Meetha Mood | Young customers, malls, online |

Remember that very common names such as "Shree Ganesh Store" can be hard to stand out with online, and generic prefixes generally don't make a name distinctive for trademark purposes. If you plan to grow beyond one shop, pick something more unique.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I choose a good shop name in Hindi?

Choose a name of two or three words that is easy to say and spell, hints at what you sell, suits your customers and area, and is not already used by a nearby shop. Test it by saying it aloud and searching it on Google Maps.

### Can I use God's name for my shop?

Yes, many Indian shops do, and it can build trust locally. But very common names like Shree Ganesh or Sai are hard to stand out with online and are difficult to protect as trademarks. Combine them with a distinctive word if you plan to grow.

### Do I need to register my shop name?

Most shops need a shop and establishment registration under their state's law, and many register on Udyam and for GST if eligible. Trademark registration is optional but useful if you want to protect the name from copycats.

### Should my shop name be in Hindi or English?

It depends on your customers. Hindi and Hinglish names feel local and friendly, while English names may suit malls or online sales. Many successful shops use a Hindi word written in English letters so it works on boards, Google Maps and WhatsApp.
