## What makes a great food business name?

- **It hints at the cuisine or vibe.** "Tandoor Tales" says North Indian; "Filter Kaapi Co." says South Indian cafe.
- **It's easy to search on delivery apps.** Customers type the first few letters, so avoid odd spellings.
- **It works as a logo.** Short names fit app thumbnails and packaging.
- **It isn't a copy of a famous brand.** Similar names can lead to legal notices and confusion.
- **It can grow with you.** "Ramesh's Momos" is limiting if you add biryani next year.

## Naming tips for Zomato and Swiggy

| Do | Avoid |
|---|---|
| Put the cuisine in the name for delivery brands ("Biryani Bandhan") | Generic names like "Food Point" that get lost in search |
| Keep it under about 20 characters so it isn't cut off | Long names that get truncated on small screens |
| Use a spelling customers will actually type | Creative spellings such as "Bryani" |
| Check that no nearby listing uses the same name | Copying a popular local brand |

Listing rules and display limits are set by each platform and can change, so check the partner guidelines when you sign up.

## Legal checks before you launch

1. **FSSAI registration or licence.** Every food business needs FSSAI registration or a licence, depending on turnover and type. Use your chosen trade name consistently on the application.
2. **Trademark screen.** Food and restaurant services generally fall under Class 43, with packaged food products in classes such as 29, 30 and 32. Use the [Trademark Class Finder](/tools/trademark-class-finder) and run a preliminary check with the [Trademark Search helper](/tools/trademark-search).
3. **Domain and Instagram handle.** Food brands live on Instagram. Check handles and domains together with the [Domain Checker](/tools/domain-checker).
4. **Shop and establishment and GST registration** where applicable, all in the same trade name.

This is general information, not legal advice. Confirm current requirements with FSSAI and your state authorities.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I choose a name for my restaurant in India?

Choose a short name that hints at your cuisine or vibe, is easy to spell and search on delivery apps, works as a logo, and doesn't copy an existing brand. Shortlist five names, check them on Zomato, Swiggy, Google Maps and Instagram, then screen trademarks in Class 43.

### What is a good name for a cloud kitchen?

For delivery-only brands, put the main dish or cuisine in the name, such as a biryani, momo or thali brand, so customers searching the app immediately understand what you sell. Keep it short enough to display fully on mobile screens.

### Can I use a Hindi name for my cafe?

Yes. Hindi and Hinglish names like Chai Charcha or Kulhad Corner feel warm and memorable. Write them in English letters so customers can search them easily on apps and Google.

### Which trademark class is for restaurants?

Restaurant, cafe and catering services generally fall under Class 43. If you also sell packaged foods or beverages, you may need classes such as 29, 30 or 32. Check the IP India classification or a professional before filing.
