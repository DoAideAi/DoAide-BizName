## What makes a business name work in India in 2026

Before the lists, four filters every shortlisted name should pass:

1. **Say it on the phone test.** If a customer hears the name once on a call, can they type it into Google or UPI without spelling help? Avoid silent letters and ambiguous spellings (Kwik vs Quick, Zeal vs Xeal).
2. **The .in or .com domain is free.** Check with the [domain availability checker](/tools/domain-checker) before you fall in love with a name. A matching domain still signals legitimacy to Indian buyers.
3. **The social handles are free.** One consistent @handle across Instagram, X and LinkedIn beats four different ones. Run it through the [social username checker](/tools/username-checker).
4. **It clears a trademark search.** A quick look at the [trademark search helper](/tools/trademark-search) saves you from a cease-and-desist in year two.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which business names are trending in India in 2026?

Short Indian-English fusion names (two syllables each part, like PaisaPath or TawaTales), Sanskrit-root names for premium brands, and invented brandable names for tech startups. Pure English dictionary words are losing ground because their domains and trademarks are mostly taken.

### Can two businesses in India have the same name?

Yes, if they are in different states or different lines of business and neither holds a trademark. But a registered trademark in your Nice class can block you nationwide, and MCA will reject a company name too similar to an existing registered company. Always run both checks before printing anything.

### Should my business name be in Hindi or English?

Match your customer. Local retail and services in Tier 2/3 cities often do better with Hindi or Hinglish names customers can say and type naturally. D2C brands and startups selling pan-India or abroad usually choose English or fusion names. Many businesses register an English legal name and trade under a Hindi brand name.

### How do I check if a business name is available in India?

Three checks: MCA company name search for the legal entity, the IP India trademark database for your product class, and a domain plus social handle check. The free tools on this site cover domains, handles and trademark classes in a few minutes.

### Is it okay to use AI to name my business?

Yes — AI generators are fastest for producing a wide shortlist. The judgment calls (cultural fit, pronunciation, legal clearance) stay with you. Treat AI output as raw material: generate 50 names, shortlist 5, verify availability, then decide.
