## Why choose a Sanskrit name for your business?

- **Built-in meaning.** Words like *Pragati* (progress) or *Nidhi* (treasure) tell a story without a tagline.
- **Trust and heritage.** Many customers associate Sanskrit words with values such as honesty, wisdom and stability.
- **Distinctiveness.** A less common Sanskrit word can be more distinctive than generic English words, which helps with MCA approval and trademarks.
- **Works across India.** Sanskrit roots are shared by many Indian languages, so the name often resonates beyond one region.

## How to make a Sanskrit name work commercially

### 1. Keep it pronounceable

Prefer two or three syllables. If customers outside your state would hesitate to say it, simplify it: *Ksheer* may become *Kheer*, or pair it with an English word.

### 2. Make it distinctive

Single common words like *Pragati* or *Shanti* are used by many existing companies. MCA's naming rules treat names that differ only by type-words, plurals or common prefixes as identical, so add a distinctive element: *Pragati Setu*, *ShantiCraft*. Read [business naming rules in India by entity type](/blog/choose-business-name-india-rules-by-entity-type).

### 3. Check the meaning carefully

Some Sanskrit words have multiple meanings, and some have religious significance that certain customers may find inappropriate for commercial use. Check with a Sanskrit dictionary or a knowledgeable person.

### 4. Check domains and trademarks

Short Sanskrit words are popular, so .com domains are often taken. Check combinations and .in alternatives with the [Domain Checker](/tools/domain-checker) and run an early screen with the [Trademark Search helper](/tools/trademark-search).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it a good idea to use a Sanskrit name for a business?

Yes, for many Indian brands. Sanskrit names carry meaning and can feel trustworthy and distinctive. Choose one that is easy to pronounce, suits your industry and isn't already widely used by other companies.

### Will MCA approve a Sanskrit company name?

MCA approves names in any language written in English letters, as long as they meet the Companies (Incorporation) Rules: not identical or too similar to existing companies, not restricted or undesirable, and consistent with your business objects. Explain the meaning clearly in the significance field.

### How do I find a unique Sanskrit name for my startup?

Start from the values or function of your product, find Sanskrit words with that meaning, then combine roots or add a modern suffix to make the name distinctive. An AI name generator can help produce combinations quickly.

### Can I trademark a Sanskrit word?

Often yes, if it is distinctive for your goods or services and doesn't conflict with existing marks. Common descriptive or religious words may be harder to register. Search the IP India trademark database before filing.
